# Episode 001 — Audio Manifest

## Dialogue

Generated per scene with neural TTS when the runtime service is available. Per-scene JSON manifests record whether voice generation succeeded and list any errors.

Character voices:
- A — natural young-adult male, dry/tired delivery
- B — energetic friendly male
- C — lower-energy deadpan male
- D — warm relaxed female

The final episode does not use meme voices, fake accents, or printed dialogue captions as a substitute for speech.

## Foley and SFX

Primary source: `SFX/Wordline_Audio_Pack_01/`.

Key reusable sounds:
- ordinary door close / latch
- body thud
- cup set-down
- tiny comedy accent
- small success ding
- sea-wave ambience

Older repository SFX are used only when Pack 01 lacks the required sound, such as the phone ring and some location ambience.

## Music

The episode currently uses an original deterministic procedural score generated by the production script. There are no third-party music license dependencies.

Sections follow the story:
- curious opening
- playful door roulette
- discovery
- hopeful plan
- donut-failure silence pocket
- faster recovery
- warm birthday payoff

The warm birthday cue intentionally continues into the tsunami reveal.

## Mix

- scene stems: dialogue / SFX / music
- 48 kHz audio
- final YouTube loudness normalization: approximately -14 LUFS integrated
- target true peak: <= -1 dBTP

The audio goal is not maximum loudness. Foley should sell physical animation while silence remains available for comedy timing.
