# Validation report - Asset Pack 03A

Generated by the build script at packaging time. Statements below describe what was actually checked.

## Automated checks (all 80 models)
- File count: 80 `.obj` and 80 `.mtl` (12 land + 6 small + 10 boats + 7 aircraft + 8 rail + 16 terrain + 12 plants + 9 rural). **PASS**
- Every OBJ re-parsed from disk: all face indices in range, no NaN/Inf vertices, `vn` count equals face count, every `usemtl` exists in the sibling MTL. **PASS (0 problems)**
- Grounding: minimum Y within 0.01 m of 0 for every model. **PASS**
- Uniqueness: no two models share a triangle-count + bounding-box signature, and no two share an identical vertex set. **PASS**
- Forward direction: for the 20 vehicle/boat/aircraft/rail models that have headlight faces, the headlight centroid lies on the -Z half of the model. **PASS (20/20)**
- Winding: each solid part was oriented outward using signed volume when it was built (heightfield terrain solids were additionally fixed after a first-pass winding bug found in preview).

## Visual inspection
Each model was rendered with a small custom software rasteriser (flat-shaded, painter's algorithm) from a three-quarter view (`previews/`). I looked at the contact sheets for every category and corrected problems I saw (inverted terrain winding, a self-intersecting cave-mouth polygon, bicycle wheels rendered as solid discs, mis-offset track wheels and hinge on two vehicles). This is *not* a Three.js/Blender render, and I did not inspect every model from every angle.

## Known limitations (honest list)
- Not edge-manifold-checked: closedness is guaranteed by construction (closed solid parts) but not verified by an edge test; parts overlap/intersect each other (typical for assembled low-poly props) rather than being boolean-merged.
- Some assets are comparatively simple: TERRAIN_01 (320 tris), BOAT_02 (640), PLANT_01, RAIL_08, RURAL_05 (about 800). Vehicles/aircraft are stylised and simplified (e.g. no interiors, flat-coloured liveries, no readable text/markings beyond shapes).
- Cave entrance has a real recessed tunnel mouth but a fairly smooth rock mass; cracked ground is separated plates over a dark slab.
- Aircraft/boats/rotors/propellers are named groups with pivots, but axes for rotation are documented only by the pivot position (rotate about the obvious axis: rotors Y, propellers/wheels X or Z as appropriate).
- Triangle counts range 320 to 11,616; AIR_02 (11.6k) and LAND_07 (10.4k) are the heaviest.
- No textures; no skeletal rigs; no LODs.
- Procedurally generated from original code; no third-party models used.
