# Wordline Audio Pack 01 — Usage Guide

This pack is designed around the new YouTube-first Wordline direction: fun, entertaining low-poly what-if animation with cinematic craft and occasional absurd running jokes.

## Default mix pattern

For most shots, use no more than three intentional layers:

1. **World** — ambience/room tone
2. **Physical** — the object/body/footstep/cloth sound
3. **Story accent** — a comedic or cinematic cue only if the beat benefits from it

## What changed

The older library leaned heavily toward:
- whooshes
- trailer impacts
- risers
- interface sounds

Pack 01 deliberately adds:
- footsteps
- chair movement
- cloth
- doors
- paper
- keys
- cups/glass/phones
- actual sip/gulp/pour utility sounds
- body thuds
- desk/chair impacts
- small comedy accents
- movement sounds

## What-if animation sound design

For normal scenes:
- let ambience breathe
- use physical Foley to sell character animation
- reserve big impacts for actual escalation

For ridiculous scenes:
- keep the world sonically believable
- the contrast between serious sound design and stupid visuals is part of the comedy

For the unnecessary tsunami:
- do not automatically use a giant meme sting
- often keep the current music playing
- let distant water/air/rumble enter late
- hard cut before the soundscape becomes generic disaster noise

## Music rule

Music is selected per episode rather than stored as one fixed channel soundtrack.

Preferred sourcing order:
1. YouTube Audio Library
2. Mixkit music
3. Pixabay only when provenance/Content ID notes are recorded
4. custom/original music when available

Every episode should record:
- track title
- creator
- source
- license/attribution requirement
- exact edit points
