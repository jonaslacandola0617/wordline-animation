# Wordline Brainrot Bible

## The 4:17 Guy

**Rule:** At exactly 4:17, an extremely ordinary office worker falls through something, lands, takes one calm sip, and launches vertically away.

**Never explain:** where he comes from, where he goes, why 4:17 matters, or why he needs the drink.

**Personality:** completely ordinary, calm, silent, and unbothered.

**Recurring audio signature:** **THUD → SIP → FWOOOP**.

**Visual rule:** no supernatural costume, glow, aura, jet flame, or monster traits. The event is absurd; the man is not.

**Continuity rule:** only lore that appears in a released episode becomes canon.
