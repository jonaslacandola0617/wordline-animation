# Asset usage — Gravity Directions

All hero meshes are loaded from the repository with OBJ + MTL and rigged using the established Wordline asset loader.

- Pack 46 — humans HUMAN_01, HUMAN_02 (hero), HUMAN_04 (umbrella businessman), HUMAN_08 (background)
- Pack 46 — glass skyscraper BUILDING_10 and neighboring BUILDING_03, BUILDING_07, BUILDING_12, BUILDING_16
- Pack 46 — CAR_01, CAR_02
- Pack 03A — LAND_01 City Bus, BOAT_01 Fishing Boat
- Pack 03B — ACCESSORY_05_Umbrella
- Pack 01 — WL_Prop_Streetlight_A, WL_Tree_Broadleaf_A
- Generated support only: city road/pavement/markings, cardboard boxes, coffee cup/drop, wave and foam geometry, clouds.

Character animation follows gravity changes and preserves the same models across shots. All camera cuts and world movement are time-derived. No narrator or overlay graphics are used.
