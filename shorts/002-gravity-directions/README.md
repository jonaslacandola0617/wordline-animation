# WORDLINE ANIMATION — Short 002

**What If Gravity Changed Direction Every 10 Seconds?**

A 40-second, colorful, visual-comedy Short using real Wordline models.

The plot is locked: coffee pulled sideways → bus drives up building → gravity up/umbrella man → cup catch/tiny sip → unnecessary tsunami → cup refill.

No narration, no explanatory graphics, no user-visible title cards.

## Production pipeline

- Three.js OBJ/MTL and articulated Wordline characters
- Exact-frame deterministic timeline and local WebGL renderer
- Headless Chromium preview PNG/JPEG stills through Playwright
- Full segmented capture on GitHub Actions
- Music, Foley and SFX from the Wordline audio system
- 48k audio master and MP4 via FFmpeg
- Master 2160×3840, social 1080×1920

## Review-first policy

Produce a contact sheet of 16 representative frames first. Do not treat renderer success as artistic approval. Move to the full 1080p preview after visual checks, then 4K master.
