# Audio direction

The soundtrack uses original procedural instrumental music and Wordline's existing, licensed-for-project-use effects (including the expanded Audio Pack 01).

It has discrete 48k stereo music, world/ambience, physical Foley and narrative SFX stems, and a final 48k mix.

Music follows the approved animation: cheerful coffee city → energetic altered gravity → music duck for the tiny sip → sincere cinematic ocean crescendo → sudden silence.

Gravity changes are marked with one reusable distinctive "DING" at exactly 10.0, 20.0, and 30.0 seconds.

The video is not dialogue-driven. Listening to the actual encoded video is required before final sign-off. A synthesized musical bed is an initial production solution, and should be replaced if its sound quality proves unconvincing.
