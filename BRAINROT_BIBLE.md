# WORDLINE ANIMATION — Brainrot Bible

## Entity: THE 4:17 GUY

**Status:** Short 001 production test.

### Canon rule
At exactly **4:17**, an ordinary office worker falls through something, lands, takes exactly one calm sip, and launches vertically away.

### Never explain
- Where he comes from.
- Where he goes.
- Why 4:17 matters.
- Why he needs one sip.
- Whether anyone knows him.
- Why the environment accepts this.

### Performance
He is completely ordinary. No dialogue. No panic. No acknowledgement of the absurdity.

### Signature rhythm
**THUD → SIP → FWOOOP**

Keep this rhythm consistent if the entity returns.

### Lore discipline
Do not add lore here unless it has appeared in a released Wordline episode. The audience should be allowed to theorize before the production invents explanations.
